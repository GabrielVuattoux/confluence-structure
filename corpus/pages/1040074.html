Terms used across the handbook

A Goods Received Note (GRN) is raised for every inbound pallet. The Temperature Excursion Report (TER) follows any breach recorded by the Temperature Log.

Where a shipment arrives without paperwork the desk raises a Hold On Receipt (HOR) and notifies the Duty Supervisor (DS).

ETA = Estimated Time of Arrival

The Pallet Exchange Scheme (PES) applies at Harbour East and Harbour West only. Note that PES also refers to the Packaging Environmental Surcharge in supplier contracts, which is unrelated.
